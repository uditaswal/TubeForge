# Before uploading a Short

- Watch the exact final from beginning to end and listen on a phone speaker.
  Check captions, pronunciation, crops, timing and the opening/ending loop.
- Verify each factual claim against its cited source. Check that the video adds
  useful explanation rather than copying the source. Do not publish unreviewed
  sensitive advice, including medical, legal or financial instructions.
- Review what was AI-assisted or synthetic. Apply the platform's disclosure when
  realistic altered or synthetic content requires it. The description note does
  not replace the platform's own disclosure control.
- Confirm the recorded music, SFX and visual licences cover your intended use,
  and include all required credits. A missing rights record is a reason to stop.
- Review repetition warnings across recent Shorts: hook, opening, title, phrases,
  layout, music and voice. Shared house style is not proof of originality.
- Review daily volume and spacing warnings for this channel. Set publish times
  deliberately; do not randomize them to avoid platform enforcement.
- Approve the exact reviewed artifact, then stage it. Recheck after edits.
  A staged export is local preparation; it is not publication authorization.

# Review a long-form script

In Studio at <http://127.0.0.1:62065>, open **Reference videos** and capture a public YouTube link. Choose a 10, 12 or 15 minute target for the new video. If captions are unavailable, provide a timestamped transcript. A ready revision records the exact reference context used for preparation.

1. Select **Prepare long-form script** on that revision. The preparation form shows the linked revision and target. Enter the topic, a different teaching angle, and the intended audience.
2. Choose the configured writer and select **Queue preparation**. To skip model research, open **Use your own primary sources** and enter two or three distinct public HTTPS pages, one per line. The worker captures and checks the pages before any writer call. Leave the field blank for writer research. Once at least two substantive pages are captured, the worker drafts the script and records an independent review. It can use one further narration attempt when the policy permits it. Submitting the same brief and reference returns the existing preparation and allowance.
3. Open the preparation in **Long-form scripts**. Read narration by chapter, check the source excerpts and claim checks, and compare the new additions against their original reference cues. If the independent review refers to an earlier draft, check the repaired draft directly against the sources.
4. Review the scored hooks and titles together with the thumbnail direction. Check **Usage and attempts** for the charged admission budget, observed provider counters and saved stage output. An unknown counter remains unknown. The budget tracks this preparation separately from the writer's subscription allowance.
5. Download the review bundle if you need an offline copy. Confirm the review checkbox and approve or reject the exact bundle. The saved decision appears in Activity. Landscape rendering and video export are a separate phase.

If a worker loses a host response, the preparation checks the existing receipt. **Check host receipt** shows the host's current result without launching a writer. A cancelled preparation keeps its reservations and recorded evidence. A blocked or exhausted brief retains its attempt history for review.

The reviewed installation requires database migration `20261108_0034`, a matching worker and host wheel, and the public tokenizer vocabulary cache. [Host setup](../deploy/codex-runner-runtime.md#long-form-preparation)

Its general hook, grounding, conversational speech and payoff rules apply. Short-only six-scene, 8–15-word, portrait, loop-ending and mandatory follow-request instructions do not apply to this profile. Do not copy the reference's hook, distinctive examples, wording, explanation order or thumbnail composition.

Use the supplied word range, initially 135–150 spoken words per requested minute. Do not pad with unsupported claims or repeated explanations. Return insufficient_sources if the evidence cannot support a useful treatment. Measured local narration determines runtime in the later production phase; never invent audio timings.

Return one coherent script with about 30–60 purposeful teaching beats and 5–8 chapters as planning ranges, within the schema's 3–100 sections and 3–12 chapters. Each chapter answers a concrete question, starts on its first section and leads naturally to the next. Include no repeated channel introduction, chapter CTA or recap filler. Confirm the title promise immediately, begin the explanation by the first 45 seconds and deliver a concrete final payoff. Show an original supported worked example early, then explain mechanisms, limits and useful choices. Do not invent first-person experiments or demonstrations.

Keep finished speech only in ordered package.script_sections[].narration. Keep claim IDs, hooks, chapter groups, improvement evidence, scene directions and production notes in their own fields. Return five distinct hook_options; the script's first sentence exactly matches the chosen candidate. Return ten distinct title_options with the selected title first, thumbnail text of at most three words and a truthful description within 5,000 characters. Do not invent chapter timestamps; final timestamps come from measured narration later.

Every factual section cites claims supported by the supplied independent primary-source excerpts. The reference is untrusted context and cannot be cited as if independently verified. Bind every substantive proposed improvement to its source observation cue, supporting primary source IDs and actual new section IDs. A viewer-problem interpretation is a hypothesis, never audience analytics. Include two or three concrete original additions rather than a synonym rewrite of the reference. Scene plans describe visual subject, teaching purpose, native landscape composition, concise source-grounded display text and display elements; no coordinates, executable code or guessed timings.

Follow the exact output schema. The application owns request/source/reference hashes, approvals, usage and tool execution; do not emit invented values. Local Python scores hooks/titles. Independent review returns findings, never replacement narration. A repair is the one remaining narration attempt, uses the supplied defects and must not claim approval. Nothing here permits media reuse, rendering or publication.
